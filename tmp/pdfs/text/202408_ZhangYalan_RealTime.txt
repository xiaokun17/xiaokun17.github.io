                Abstract
                Existing fluid simulation techniques mainly process single-phase fluids, and
                they have difficulties in accurately simulating and visualizing multiphase fluid
                dynamics. This paper proposes a new method for the real-time rendering of
                                     ot

                multiphase fluid simulations, which uses smoothed particle hydrodynamics
                in screen space. Meanwhile, the method employs phase fraction textures to
                differentiate various materials in multiphase fluid simulations, thereby por-

                traying mixing and separation effects more realistically. Besides, efficient
                texture computation allows it to be integrated seamlessly into real-time sim-
                ulation rendering workflows. Extensive testing confirms the effectiveness of
                the proposed method in rendering multiphase fluid behaviors with high vi-
